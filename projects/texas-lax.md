I design the apparel, uniforms and merchandise for Texas Men's Lacrosse. I started as Assistant Equipment Manager in April 2025, and I was given full control over certain projects, like the Longhorn Shootout merch and the Shootout for Soldiers uniforms. I wasn't yet in charge of ordering or overseeing everything the team wore. In April 2026 I was promoted to Equipment Manager. Now I'm in charge of everything the team wears, including ordering, inventory and distribution for a roster of about 50 players, and I have an assistant of my own.

- **Longhorn Shootout.** The annual tournament the team hosts for K-12 teams. I design and sell the merch there. It brought in $55K in onsite merchandise revenue at the 2026 tournament, and the best seller was the youth-size custom tournament shirt, a Nike Dri-FIT I designed with that year's tournament logo on the front.
- **Shootout for Soldiers.** Military-themed jerseys for Texas and rival Texas A&M in a charity game honoring veterans. Each team's uniform honored a specific alum. For A&M, I wanted the colors to resemble the Corps of Cadets uniform. For Texas, I wanted to find a way to make camo work with burnt orange, since UT is fairly limited in the colors we're allowed to use, and an arctic camo palette looked best with it. The tradeoff was that we didn't have a specific UT alum who fought in an arctic setting to tie it to. The game raised $1,500 through online and in-person donations.
- **Pink Out (upcoming).** Custom uniforms (jerseys and shorts) and helmet decals for a breast cancer awareness game on February 19, 2027. The uniforms are mostly white with pink accents, and I'm planning a media campaign around the game.

**Process.** I have full creative control over our designs. BSN makes our uniforms and most of the team's gear, anything Nike, and my relationship with our BSN rep brings in $10,000 a year in free ordering. This year we're also working with Austin Screen Printing on Comfort Colors T-shirts and embroidered hats to sell at the tournament.
