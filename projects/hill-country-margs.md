I worked at Hill Country Margaritas, a margarita machine rental company in Austin, setting up and picking up machines at customers' homes and events. Most of the people I delivered to were young and cool, and they loved the brand, but there was no merch they could buy. I set out to design a line for that audience: people in their 20s, fans of the brand, people who like drinking but not in a sad way.

**This is a concept project. These designs were never put into production or sold.**

**The concept.** I kept the brand's existing green and paired it with black. The star motif came from Hollywood inspiration, because I think Austin is a young Hollywood. I wanted the line to feel western in an Austin way: a version of western that's a little more streetwear and elevated.

**The pieces.** The line has eight pieces: one hoodie, three shirts, three hats, and a dog toy. Some of them have nothing to do with drinking. I made them anyway because they fit the brand's vibe.

**Process.** I got every idea I had out first, then started getting creative. I worked with new tools, including different features in Canva, and kept pushing the Hill Country theme, then started thinking outside the box.
