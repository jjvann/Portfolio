Beyond Malibu is a Young Life camp in British Columbia with a rich culture, and I know it from the inside as a mountain guide leading backpacking and sea kayaking trips. Next summer I'm taking over the camp shop, and I'm redesigning its brand and merchandise from the ground up.

**The problem.** The current shop is outdated. It's full of items with repetitive, boring logos, and the stickers don't match each other. None of it has the creativity or life of the camp, and the old merch doesn't do its culture justice.

**What I'm building.** A new brand identity and a more creative, higher-quality merch line, starting with a beanie, shirts, and a hoodie. I'm also redesigning the stickers. Each backpacking route we guide, one per mountain, has its own sticker, but they come in different sizes and use different fonts. I'm making them one uniform set so they read as a collection.

**My approach.** The goal is for what campers take home to match the quality of the experience they had. I'm drawing on the camp's culture and personality so the merch feels like the camp.

**Status.** This project is in progress and is planned to launch in Summer 2027. Process work and designs will be added here as they take shape.
