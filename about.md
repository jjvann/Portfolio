I'm a senior at UT Austin studying Informatics with a concentration in UX Design, graduating in May 2027. My UX training taught me to start with the people I'm designing for, and what I love most is turning that into things people actually wear and keep, from team uniforms to full merch lines. I'm the Equipment Manager for Texas Men's Lacrosse, where I design our gear and merch, and I'm a seasonal mountain guide at Beyond Malibu, a Young Life camp in British Columbia, where I'll be taking over the camp shop next summer. Outside of design, I'm usually backpacking, camping, or playing sports. I'm looking for apparel, merch, or product design roles where I can help shape how a brand looks, feels, and gets worn.
